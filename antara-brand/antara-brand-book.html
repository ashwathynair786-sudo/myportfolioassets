The name

What Antara means

The name comes from Sanskrit and from Indian classical music. Both meanings point the same way: inward, and then upward.

अंतरा
antarā · /ən̪.t̪ə.ɾaː/
Within
From the Sanskrit antar: inner, inside, the space within. An Antara light glows from inside its shade, through paper, silk, fabric or cane, never straight from a bare bulb.
The rising verse
In a raga or a song, the antara is the second movement. After the sthayi settles low, the antara climbs into the upper octave. It is the moment a song lifts, and the moment a room changes when the lights come on in the evening.
The space between
Antar also means interval, the gap between two things. Light lives in that gap, between the shade and the room it falls on.

Logo meaning

Three lights in one name

The logo is the name ANTARA set in Alta, with its three A's redrawn as hanging pendants. Each one carries a small bulb where the crossbar used to be, so the name holds three lights, the way a room holds three pendants over a table.

The mark: one pendant A

The pendant A

Each A is drawn as a hanging shade. The apex is where the cord meets the shade, and the two legs are its sides, the thin one and the thick one kept from Alta.

The bulb

A small half circle hangs where the crossbar was. It is Ember on paper and Glow when lit. It is the only coloured part of the logo, just as light is the only coloured part of the illustrations.

The space within

Taking out the crossbar opens the inside of the letter, the antar. The bar that used to close the A is now the light inside it.

Three lights

ANTARA has three A's, so the name holds three lights, evenly spaced like pendants in a row. In the hanging version they drop from one line, as if from a ceiling.

The other letters

N, T and R stay in Alta, unchanged: tall, light and widely spaced. They are the room. The A's are the lights in it.

The tagline

"Light from within" in a warm cream serif. It names what every Antara piece does, and now what the logo does too.

Switch the bulbs off and the letters still stand. Switch them on and the name comes alive. The structure is always there; the light is what makes it Antara.

Brand philosophy

Light is better when it passes through something. We shape paper, silk, fabric and cane by hand, so every room finds its antara.

The material makes the mood

Rice paper, raw silk, crushed fabric and woven cane each soften light in their own way. The shade matters more than the bulb.

Warmth before brightness

We choose amber over white and glow over glare. A good light makes a room feel lived in, not lit up.

Every glow has a direction

A pendant pools light on a table. A sconce washes a wall. Each piece is designed for the place it hangs.

Made by hand

Pleats, ribs, fringes and cane weave are finished by hand, so no two pieces glow exactly alike.

Illustration · The lights

Twelve forms of light

The illustration system draws every form in the Antara range, grouped by material. Each light is drawn as a border only. When it is switched on, the material glows from within. Tap any light to switch it off or on.

Line

One 1.5 px outline in Ash on dark, or Soot on paper. Ribs, pleats, fringe and weave use a finer line.

Glow

Shades are translucent, so the whole shade fills with light: near white at the centre, Filament at the edges.

Direction

Open shades pool light below. Sconces wash up and down the wall. Closed forms glow all round.

Motion

Lights flicker on and switch off. They never fade slowly, because real lights don't.

Logo usage

Versions of the logo

The Pendant A wordmark is the primary logo. Use the lit version on dark grounds, the hanging version where there is room for drama, and the mark where the wordmark would be too small. The lamp scene stays on as a key visual.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of an A from its tip to the bulb. Nothing else enters that space.

Minimum size

Wordmark: 30 mm or 160 px wide, so the bulbs stay visible. Below that, use the mark. Mark: 16 px. Drop the tagline below 50 mm or 260 px.

Don't

  • Put the crossbars back, or fill the A's.
  • Colour the bulbs white, blue or any cool colour.
  • Use the lit glow on a light background.
  • Hang the A's at different heights.
  • Retype ANTARA in Alta. Always use the logo file.

Colour

Use roughly these proportions. Most of any layout is dark. Light colours are used like light: in small, warm amounts.

Typography

Three typefaces, three jobs

Alta is kept for the logo. Cormorant Garamond is the voice of headlines, product names and quotes. Montserrat does the quiet work of body text, specifications and labels.

Logo only · custom

Alta, redrawn

The wordmark starts from Alta, a tall, open display face, with the three A's redrawn as pendants. Never retype the name or set new words in Alta. Always use the logo file.
