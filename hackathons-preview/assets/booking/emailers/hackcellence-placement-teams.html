Dear Placement Team,

Most internship selection processes begin by making the pile smaller: CGPA cut-offs, college tiering, or keyword scans on resumes. It's an efficient system but it often means talented engineers are filtered out before anyone sees a single line of their code.

For their 2027 internship intake, the Booking Holdings India Center of Excellence is taking a different approach. HackCellence: The Embark Edition, hosted on HackerEarth, has zero upfront shortlisting. Any engineering student graduating in 2027 can enter, and their progress depends entirely on what they build.

 
How it runs
Online Rounds: Two online coding rounds, each an elimination.
On-Site Finale: Candidates who clear both rounds spend two days at the Booking Holdings Bangalore office, building alongside their engineering teams.
The Internship: Top performers from the finale secure a six-month internship (January to June 2027) at ₹75,000/month.
 
What students need to know
Eligibility: Open to all engineering students graduating in 2027 (any branch, any college, any CGPA) available on-site in Bangalore for the internship.
Low Friction: Individual registration only—no team to assemble, no prior hackathon experience required, and nothing to prepare before signing up.
