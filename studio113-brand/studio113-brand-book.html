No. 113 Park Street · Calcutta House of Kanchana Est. 1967

Story time

Lady K is the face of Studio 113. She steps out of a vintage mural that has hung in the House of Kanchana store on Park Street, Calcutta, for decades. The House has dressed the city since 1967, and Studio 113 is its newest label.

The logo turns her into a postage stamp, after the ornate frames of vintage Indian stamps: her profile inside an arched window, a flower in her hair, 113 PS as the stamp's value and House of Kanchana as its mark of origin. Like a letter, every saree that leaves the studio carries her with it.

Logo grid

Module
The frame is a 4 : 5 rectangle, 16 modules wide and 20 tall. Every element snaps to this grid.
Vertical axis
Lady K, the arch and the banner are centred on one axis. Only her profile turns away from it.
Concentric arch
The window and the arched name share one centre. Studio 113 is set along the outer circle.
Bands
The 113 PS tags sit on the centre band, 1.5 modules tall. The House of Kanchana banner is 11 modules wide and 1.75 modules tall.
