An identity for a luxury Indian fashion label, drawn from the intricate frames of vintage Indian postage stamps.

The brief
113Studio · House of Kanchana

An identity that honours tradition, celebrates artistry and carries elegance into every touchpoint.

Studio 113 needed a mark as refined as its drapes: rooted in Indian craft, quiet enough for luxury, and distinctive enough to be recognised at a glance.
